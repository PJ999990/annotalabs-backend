Frequently Asked Questions

What is Annotalabs?

Annotalabs is a data annotation company specializing in production-grade training data for artificial intelligence and machine learning models. We transform raw data into structured, labeled datasets across text, image, audio, video, and generative AI modalities.

What services does Annotalabs provide?

We provide image annotation, video tracking, text classification, audio transcription, 3D point cloud labeling, and RLHF preference ranking.

Why should companies outsource data annotation to Annotalabs?

Because we combine a dedicated workforce with a strict multi-layer QA framework. We don't use crowdsourcing; we use domain-trained specialists who learn your specific guidelines.

How does Annotalabs ensure annotation quality?

Every dataset passes through three layers: annotator self-review, peer review, and senior QA spot-checks. We maintain a 99.2% accuracy standard.
