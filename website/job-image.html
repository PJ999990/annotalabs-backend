AI Image Reviewer — Data Annotation & Quality

Review and validate image datasets, ensuring high-quality annotations that train the next generation of computer vision models.

The Work

You will help prepare image data for AI systems by reviewing existing annotations and verifying their accuracy. This role is perfect for individuals with a sharp eye for detail who are comfortable navigating web-based tools.

  • Review image annotations for accuracy, consistency, and completeness.
  • Identify and correct annotation errors using detailed project guidelines.
  • Maintain consistent quality across a high volume of image data.
  • Communicate questions, challenges, and potential issues to the project team.
  • Meet project deadlines, including accelerated timelines when required.

What It Pays and Takes

Pay Rate: $40 - $52 per hour

This is a full-time contract role with a minimum time commitment of 20 hours per week.

  • Work arrangement: Remote and must reside in the US or Canada
  • Time requirement: 25 hours per week.
  • Role type: Full-time contract work.
  • Language: English.
  • Experience: Entry-level (0 years). No previous professional annotation experience required.
  • Skills: Proficiency in computer and internet use, strong attention to detail, and the ability to follow detailed instructions consistently.
  • Equipment: Provided by Annotalabs

**Must have reliable internet connection**
