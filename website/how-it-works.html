From Raw Data to AI-Ready Datasets

Our methodology blends technology, skilled annotators, and secure workflows, ensuring every dataset is accurate, enterprise-ready, and tailored for industry-specific AI applications.

Step 1

Consult & Define

We begin by thoroughly understanding your goals, data requirements, and model architecture. Then, we collaboratively define clear annotation guidelines to ensure consistency and accuracy in every outcome.

  • Kickoff call to align on project scope
  • Custom taxonomy & guideline creation
  • Secure data transfer setup (S3, SFTP, API)
Step 2

Annotate & Review

Your data is processed by our domain-trained teams. We start with a pilot batch for your approval, then scale to full production. Every label is applied with multi-stage quality checks.

  • Pilot batch delivery within 48 hours
  • Dedicated annotator teams assigned to your project
  • Continuous feedback loops and guideline refinements
Step 3

Multi-Layer Quality Assurance

Before a single label is delivered to you, it passes through our rigorous three-layer QA framework designed to catch edge cases, inconsistencies, and errors.

  • Annotator self-review and peer review
  • Senior QA manager spot-checks
  • Automated schema and boundary validations
Step 4

Deliver & Scale

Receive your production-ready datasets in any format you need (COCO, YOLO, JSON, CSV). As your model evolves and your data requirements grow, our workforce scales with you.

  • Delivery via secure cloud or API
  • Flexible formatting to match your ML pipeline
  • Ongoing support for continuous model training

Why our accuracy is 99.2%

We don't rely on automated checks alone. Every dataset is human-verified to ensure your models train on the highest quality data possible.

🔍

Dual-Pass Review

Every task is annotated by one worker and verified by another. Disagreements trigger a senior review.

📊

Agreement Scoring

We track inter-annotator agreement. If it drops below target, we retrain the team immediately.

⚙️

Automated Checks

Schema validation and rule-based checks catch obvious errors before human review even begins.
