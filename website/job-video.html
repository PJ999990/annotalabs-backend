Data Annotator — Video & 3D LiDAR

Annotate video footage by creating and reviewing bounding boxes, correcting errors, and following detailed project guidelines.

The Work

You will help prepare video data for AI systems by marking relevant objects and checking that existing annotations are accurate. The work requires careful attention across a high volume of footage and clear communication when questions or issues arise.

  • Create accurate bounding boxes around relevant objects in video footage.
  • Review video annotations for accuracy, consistency, and completeness.
  • Identify and correct annotation errors using detailed project guidelines.
  • Maintain consistent quality across a high volume of video data.
  • Communicate questions, challenges, and potential issues to the project team.
  • Meet project deadlines, including accelerated timelines when required.

What It Pays and Takes

Pay Rate: $50 - $74 per hour

This is a full-time contract role with a minimum time commitment of 20 hours per week.

  • Work arrangement: Remote and must reside in the US or Canada
  • Time requirement: 25 hours per week.
  • Role type: full-time contract work.
  • Language: English.
  • Experience: At least 0-1 years of experience in data annotation projects.
  • Required background: Previous experience with video-based data annotation and bounding boxes or similar object annotation tasks.
  • Skills: Strong attention to detail, visual quality checking, and the ability to follow detailed instructions consistently.
  • Equipment: Provided by Annotalabs

**Must have reliable internet connection**
