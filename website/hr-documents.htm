Welcome to Annotalabs

Welcome to the team! We are a remote-first company dedicated to powering the next generation of AI models with high-quality, human-verified data. This handbook provides a quick overview of our expectations and policies for Data Annotators.

1. Remote Work Environment

You are responsible for maintaining a quiet, dedicated workspace free from interruptions. Because you are handling potentially sensitive data (e.g., personal images, medical records, or confidential video), your screen must be kept private. A privacy screen filter is recommended if you work in a shared space.

2. Time Tracking and Availability

Your standard work week is 25 hours. You must track your hours using our internal portal. Core working hours (when you must be online and responsive to messages) are [e.g., 9:00 AM - 3:00 PM EST]. If you need to adjust your schedule, please coordinate with your Team Manager at least 24 hours in advance.

3. Communication Standards

We rely heavily on asynchronous communication. You are expected to:

  • Check your Slack and email at least three times during your shift.
  • Flag any confusing images, edge cases, or guideline contradictions to your QA Lead immediately.
  • Respond to direct messages from your Training Agent or Team Manager within 2 hours during core hours.

4. Quality Assurance (QA) Policy

Our clients expect 99.2% accuracy. To maintain this, all annotators are subject to random QA audits. If your accuracy drops below 95%, you will be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) which includes additional training with your assigned Agent. Repeated quality failures may result in removal from a project.

5. Security & Equipment

All client data is confidential. You must never download client datasets to your personal devices. Only use the company-provided laptop and the YubiKey for authentication. Report any lost or stolen equipment immediately to IT and HR. We take data privacy seriously; violations of the NDA will result in immediate termination.

6. Paid Time Off (PTO) and Scheduling

Because we operate as a contract workforce aligned to client deadlines, we ask that you request PTO at least 2 weeks in advance. Unapproved absences during critical project sprints may impact your contract renewal. All requests should go through your Team Manager.
